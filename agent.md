# Answering by talking

This workbook asks for a lot of writing. If typing at length is difficult — or simply not how
you think best — you can answer by talking to an assistant instead, and bring the answers back
here.

## How it works

Turn it on below. Every question then grows a small button that copies that question, written
out as a brief, to your clipboard. You paste it into an assistant of your choosing, it
interviews you about that one question, and it gives you back a block of text. You paste that
block back here and review it before anything is saved.

Nothing happens automatically, and nothing happens to a question you do not ask about.

## What leaves this device, and what does not

**This application cannot send anything anywhere.** That is not a promise about what it
chooses to do — it is a restriction the browser enforces on it, and a test fails the build if
it ever weakens.

What reaches an assistant is what you paste into it. Before you copy anything you are shown
the exact text, in full, so the decision is one you can actually make. Your existing answers
are never included unless you ask for them, one question at a time.

Whether to hand this material to an assistant is your call. It is worth knowing that most
assistants keep what you type, and that this workbook is about the most personal things you
are likely to write down.

## If you would rather not

Leave it off. The worksheets are unchanged, and nothing on any page will mention it again.
