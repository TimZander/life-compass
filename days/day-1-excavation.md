# Day 1 — Excavation

**Time:** ~80 min · **Goal:** gather raw material from your own life.

The goal today is **not** to draw conclusions. It's to gather data that the later
days will draw on. Resist the urge to synthesize — just collect.

---

## 1. Life chapters timeline (20 min)

Divide your life into 5–8 chapters. For each, write a title, one sentence on what
defined it, and one sentence on what you learned.

> Example title: "The garage-band years," "First job that mattered."

<!-- questions: day1.chapters -->

---

## 2. Peak experiences (20 min)

List 5 moments where you felt most alive, most yourself, most in flow. They don't need
to be big — a specific day works better than a general era.

For each: What was I doing? Who was I with (or was I alone)? What was the underlying
quality — challenge, creation, connection, mastery, service, freedom, beauty, something
else?

<!-- questions: day1.peaks -->

---

## 3. Low points (15 min)

List 3 hard chapters or moments. For each: What was being violated or missing? What did
this teach me about what I need or won't accept?
