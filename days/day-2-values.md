# Day 2 — Values

**Time:** ~65 min · **Goal:** name what you stand for.

Values are **not aspirations.** They're the principles you actually use to evaluate
whether a life feels right. Today you'll narrow a long list down to 5 ranked,
operationalized values.

---

## 2. Narrow to 10 (10 min)

From your circled list, pick the 10 that feel most non-negotiable.

> The test: if a value were taken from your life, would it feel like a loss of self?

<!-- questions: day2.shortlist_ten -->

## 3. Narrow to 5 (15 min)

The hardest step. Force-rank down to 5. When two compete, ask: **which one, if I had to
give it up, would hurt more?**

<!-- questions: day2.shortlist_five -->

---

## 4. Operationalize (15 min)

For each of your 5, write a one-sentence definition **in your own words** (not the
dictionary's), what it looks like when you're living it, and what it looks like when
you're betraying it.

<!-- questions: day2.operationalised -->

---

## 5. Conflict test (10 min)

Values clash in real decisions. Take your ranking from step 3 and test it against **3
recent hard decisions** you made. Does your stated ranking match how you actually chose?
If not, your stated ranking is wrong — adjust it.
